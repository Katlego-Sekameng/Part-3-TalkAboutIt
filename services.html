Our Services

For Clients

Our primary service is connecting individuals who need mental health support with the best qualified professionals in the field. We ensure that these professionals can connect with clients regardless of geographical distance, making mental health support accessible to everyone.

Clients have the freedom to choose any professional they wish to book based on their specific needs and the professional's expertise. Our transparent pricing model ensures that you know exactly what you're paying for quality mental health support.

For Mental Health Professionals

TalkAboutIt provides a unique opportunity for psychology graduates and mental health professionals to build their practice and gain valuable work experience. You set your own rates and control your schedule.

We keep a record of all clients you've served through your personal professional dashboard, which is useful for future job applications.

Our revenue-sharing model is fair: TalkAboutIt takes 30% of your service fees to maintain the platform, while you earn 70% directly, calculated monthly.
